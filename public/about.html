About CareerPath Finder

Helping Students Discover Their Future

Our Purpose

CareerPath Finder is an interactive website designed to help high school and college students explore potential career opportunities based on their interests, strengths, and educational goals.

Choosing a career can be challenging. Our goal is to make career exploration simple, engaging, and accessible to students who are beginning to think about their futures.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower students with information and resources that encourage them to explore careers aligned with their personal interests and strengths.

Our Vision

Our vision is to help students develop confidence in exploring educational and professional opportunities while preparing for their future careers.

How CareerPath Finder Works

1. Take the Quiz

Answer questions about your favorite subjects, interests, strengths, and educational goals.

2. Discover Careers

Receive career suggestions based on your questionnaire responses and areas of interest.

3. Explore Your Future

Learn about career responsibilities, relevant skills, and educational requirements.
